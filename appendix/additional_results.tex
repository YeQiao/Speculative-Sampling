\paragraph{GPU-only speedup breakdown.}
On the H100 NVL, the AR baseline runs at $\sim$76\,tok/s.
With Mamba2-27M guided, speculative throughput is $\sim$37\,tok/s ($0.49\times$); with LLaMA-1B drafter, $\sim$67\,tok/s ($0.83\times$).
The speedup is below 1$\times$ because verification already takes only 16\,ms on this fast GPU; any drafter overhead degrades the pipeline.
This motivates our CPU-offloading approach where draft latency is hidden behind GPU verification.
