\paragraph{Guidance substantially improves SSM acceptance.}
Our guided Mamba2-27M drafter achieves a mean of 2.33 accepted tokens per round, a 25\% improvement over the pretrained baseline (1.86) and 40\% over the KD-only variant with guidance zeroed out (1.66).
The guidance benefit is consistent across all five benchmarks, with the largest gains on GSM8K (+0.82) and HumanEval (+0.58).
Code generation (HumanEval) and math reasoning (GSM8K) yield the highest guided acceptance, likely because their token distributions are more structured.
Summarization (XSum) is hardest across all drafters, consistent with its requirement for creative paraphrasing.

\paragraph{KD without guidance hurts under masking.}
A surprising finding is that the knowledge-distilled drafter with guidance zeroed out (1.66) performs \emph{worse} than the pretrained drafter (1.86).
This indicates that the KD-trained backbone has become reliant on the guidance delta signal: when guidance is removed, the backbone's predictions are miscalibrated.
This highlights the importance of evaluating guided models both with and without their guidance pathway active.

\paragraph{Mamba2 vs.\ transformer drafter.}
The LLaMA-3.2-1B transformer drafter achieves a higher mean acceptance (3.39) than our guided Mamba2-27M (2.33), which is expected given its $44\times$ larger backbone parameter count.
However, the transformer drafter's advantage must be weighed against its inference cost: LLaMA-1B requires substantially more memory and compute per draft token, and its KV-cache grows linearly with context length.
On an H100 GPU, both drafters result in throughput below the 75.8~tok/s autoregressive baseline because verification dominates latency at this hardware scale.
The SSM drafter's real advantage materializes in the CPU-offloaded pipeline (\S\ref{sec:cpu_results}), where its tiny size enables efficient heterogeneous execution.

\paragraph{Verifier scaling.}
With the larger LLaMA-3.1-70B verifier (80 layers, 8-bit quantized), pretrained Mamba2-27M acceptance improves from 1.86 to 2.51, a 35\% increase.
This suggests that guidance benefits compound with verifier quality, since a more capable verifier provides richer hidden-state signals.
