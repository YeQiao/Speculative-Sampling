We presented SpecSSM, a speculative decoding framework that pairs tiny externally pretrained Mamba2 drafters with transformer verifiers of varying scale and family.
By injecting verifier hidden states into the SSM's state-update pathway via lightweight linear projections, guided Mamba2-45M achieves 2.82 mean accepted tokens per round with LLaMA-8B (85\% over the unguided baseline), and guided Mamba2-27M achieves 2.33 (+25\%).
Guidance transfers across verifier families (LLaMA, Gemma) and scales with verifier size: pretrained acceptance increases 35\% from LLaMA-8B to LLaMA-70B.
As a released internal baseline, chain-only EAGLE-3 reaches 1.41 mean accepted tokens with its matched LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct verifier, remaining below both guided SSM variants.

We introduced activation replay as an efficient method to resynchronize the SSM's recurrent state after speculative rejection, replacing $O(T)$ re-prefill with $O(K)$ replay.
On CPU, this yields up to $201{\times}$ speedup at 1024-token contexts, making it essential for the CPU-offloaded pipeline.
Our profiling-guided kernel optimization stack (AVX-512 $\to$ fused BF16 $\to$ INT8 VNNI) achieves $7.5{\times}$ over na\"ive PyTorch by targeting the linear-projection bottleneck that dominates 85\% of SSM inference time, completing 8-token drafts in 9\,ms, well within the 16\,ms GPU verification window.
The projected pipeline throughput of 202\,tok/s represents $2.7{\times}$ over autoregressive decoding.

Our ablation analysis reveals several practical insights: backbone finetuning provides $3\times$ the benefit of guidance projection alone, a single high verifier layer is nearly as effective as three, and KD is not a prerequisite for guidance training.

\paragraph{Future work.}
(1)~Implement and benchmark the full asynchronous CPU-GPU pipeline end-to-end;
(2)~context length scaling experiments to quantify replay and throughput benefits over longer generations;
(3)~evaluate the 70B-guided checkpoint now completing training;
(4)~tree-structured drafting to further improve acceptance at the cost of verification complexity.
