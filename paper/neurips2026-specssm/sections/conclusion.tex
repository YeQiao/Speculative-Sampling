We presented SpecSSM, a speculative decoding framework that pairs a tiny 65M-parameter Mamba2 drafter with a LLaMA-3.1-8B verifier.
By injecting verifier hidden states into the SSM's state-update pathway, our guided drafter achieves an average of 3.17 accepted tokens per speculation round across five diverse benchmarks, while being $\sim$20$\times$ smaller than the smallest comparable transformer drafter.
We introduced activation replay as an efficient method to resynchronize the SSM's recurrent state after speculative rejection, avoiding costly full re-prefill.

Our analysis of rejection masking revealed a previously undocumented interaction between non-compact speculative layouts and recurrent drafters, where omitting masking inflates acceptance metrics for SSM drafters while deflating them for transformer drafters.

\paragraph{Future work.}
(1)~CPU-offloaded drafting with custom SSM kernels for memory-constrained deployment;
(2)~scaling the guided SSM drafter to 130M--370M parameters;
(3)~tree-structured drafting to amplify the benefit of each speculation round.
