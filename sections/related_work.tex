\citet{leviathan2023fast} and \citet{chen2023accelerating} independently showed that a lightweight drafter can propose multiple tokens verified in a single target-model forward pass, preserving the output distribution while reducing latency.
The design space has since expanded along two axes: \emph{where} the drafter lives and \emph{how} it is aligned with the verifier.
Self-drafting methods reuse verifier components---Medusa~\citep{cai2024medusa} attaches parallel heads, Draft~\&~Verify~\citep{zhang2024draft} exploits early exits, EAGLE~\citep{li2024eagle} and EAGLE-3~\citep{li2024eagle3} autoregressively predict verifier features, and SpecInfer~\citep{miao2024specinfer} explores tree-structured candidates---but all share GPU memory with the verifier and require architectural coupling.
External drafters avoid this coupling: DistillSpec~\citep{zhou2023distillspec} aligns a separate transformer drafter via knowledge distillation, and \citet{berdoz2026steering} inject verifier hidden states as steering vectors at inference time (+35\% acceptance).
Our guided injection extends this inference-time alignment to SSM architectures, where the recurrent state-update pathway demands a fundamentally different integration strategy than transformer attention residuals.

On the SSM side, Mamba~\citep{gu2023mamba} introduced input-dependent selective state spaces achieving transformer-competitive perplexity at linear complexity, and Mamba2~\citep{dao2024transformers} reformulated this as structured state space duality (SSD) with multi-head structure.
\citet{choi2025mamba} first explored Mamba as a speculative drafter, demonstrating competitive acceptance rates and proposing tree search for draft quality, but left unaddressed the challenges of verifier alignment, post-rejection state resynchronization, and efficient deployment.

SpecSSM addresses all three gaps simultaneously: we inject verifier hidden states into the Mamba2 recurrence to bridge the large architectural gap (+88\% over unguided), introduce activation replay for $O(K)$ state resynchronization after rejection (vs.\ $O(T)$ re-prefill), and offload the entire drafter to CPU via a custom AVX-512/INT8 kernel stack that fits within the GPU verification window.
