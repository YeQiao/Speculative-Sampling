\paragraph{Speculative decoding.}
Speculative decoding was introduced independently by \citet{leviathan2023fast} and \citet{chen2023accelerating}, who showed that a small transformer drafter can accelerate a larger verifier without changing the output distribution.
Subsequent work has explored tree-based verification~\citep{miao2024specinfer}, self-speculation from intermediate exits~\citep{zhang2024draft}, and Medusa-style parallel heads~\citep{cai2024medusa}.
EAGLE~\citep{li2024eagle} uses second-to-top-layer features from the target model to predict next-token features, achieving strong speedups through feature-level autoregression.
\citet{berdoz2026steering} inject verifier hidden states into pretrained transformer drafters via steering vectors, improving acceptance rates by up to 35\%.
Knowledge distillation has also been applied to align drafters with verifiers~\citep{zhou2023distillspec}.

\paragraph{Draft model architectures.}
Most speculative decoding systems use distilled or independently trained transformer drafters, typically 100M to 1B parameters~\citep{leviathan2023fast, zhou2023distillspec}.
Recent work has explored non-transformer drafters: \citet{choi2025mamba} demonstrated that Mamba-based SSM models achieve competitive acceptance rates as drafters despite lacking attention, and further proposed tree-structured search for draft quality.

\paragraph{State space models for language.}
Mamba~\citep{gu2023mamba} introduced input-dependent selective state spaces for language modeling, achieving transformer-competitive perplexity at linear complexity.
Mamba2~\citep{dao2024transformers} reformulated the selective scan as a structured state space duality (SSD) with multi-head structure, enabling efficient hardware-aware implementations.
Hybrid architectures combining SSM layers with attention~\citep{lieber2024jamba} have also shown promise.

\paragraph{Positioning.}
Our work is closest to \citet{choi2025mamba} (Mamba drafter for speculative decoding) and \citet{berdoz2026steering} (guided speculation via verifier hidden states).
We combine both ideas, injecting verifier signals into an SSM drafter, while additionally addressing SSM cache resynchronization via activation replay and proposing CPU offloading with custom INT8 kernels for memory-constrained deployment.
Unlike prior guided approaches that target transformer drafters, we show that guidance is effective for recurrent models where no attention mechanism exists to directly align.
