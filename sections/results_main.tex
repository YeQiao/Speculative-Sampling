\paragraph{Guided Mamba2-65M achieves strong acceptance.}
Our guided drafter achieves an average of 3.17 accepted tokens per round across all benchmarks, with per-dataset acceptance ranging from 2.42 (XSum) to 3.75 (HumanEval).
Code generation (HumanEval) and instruction-following (Alpaca) yield the highest acceptance, likely because their token distributions are more predictable.
Summarization (XSum) is hardest, consistent with its requirement for creative paraphrasing.
