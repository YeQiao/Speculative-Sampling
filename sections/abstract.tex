Speculative decoding accelerates large language model inference by drafting tokens with a fast model and verifying them in parallel.
Self-drafting methods reuse verifier components but consume GPU memory, require verifier modifications, and degrade on out-of-distribution inputs; transformer-based external drafters are more robust yet carry quadratic attention costs and large KV caches.
We present \textbf{SpecSSM}, which pairs a tiny externally pretrained Mamba2 drafter (27M backbone parameters, $44\times$ smaller than LLaMA-3.2-1B) with verifier-guided hidden-state injection, \emph{activation replay} for $O(K)$ SSM state resynchronization after rejection, and a CPU-offloaded pipeline with a custom AVX-512/INT8 kernel that completes 8-token drafts in 9\,ms.
Across five benchmarks with LLaMA-3.1-8B, guidance improves mean acceptance from 1.86 to 2.33 tokens per round (+25\%), scaling to 2.51 with LLaMA-70B, and the CPU-GPU pipeline projects $2.7\times$ throughput over autoregressive decoding while freeing all GPU memory for the verifier.
