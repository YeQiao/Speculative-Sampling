\paragraph{Model scope.}
We evaluate only one verifier (LLaMA-3.1-8B) and one drafter size (Mamba2-65M).  Generalization to other verifier families (e.g., Mistral, Qwen) and larger SSM drafters remains to be validated.

\paragraph{Training regime.}
The guided drafter was trained with knowledge distillation from the verifier on the FineWeb-Edu distribution.  Domain shift between pretraining data and evaluation benchmarks may affect acceptance rates.

\paragraph{Masked vs.\ unmasked evaluation.}
Our main results use unmasked evaluation because the checkpoint was trained in this setting.  While we show this inflates acceptance for SSM drafters, mask-aware retraining may close the gap.

\paragraph{Systems evaluation.}
We report acceptance rates and throughput but have not yet completed comprehensive wall-clock speedup measurements or CPU offloading experiments.

\paragraph{Broader impact.}
Speculative decoding accelerates text generation, which could amplify both beneficial and harmful uses of language models.  Our work does not introduce new capabilities beyond speed; all outputs are provably identical to the verifier's distribution.
